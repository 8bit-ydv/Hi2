# Sovereign of the Three Realms — Chapter → Comic Image Generator

**How to use this file:** Attach this file to a message and type **only the chapter number** (e.g. `483` or `Chapter 484`). No other instructions needed — follow everything below automatically.

---

## STEP 1 — Get the chapter text
Novel: *Sovereign of the Three Realms* (तीनों लोकों का संप्रभु) by Li Tian.
- Search the web for: `Sovereign of the Three Realms Chapter <N>` and fetch the full raw chapter text (novelhall.com mirrors work well; URL pattern is usually found via web_search since chapter IDs aren't sequential).
- Read the ENTIRE chapter (fetch all pagination/chunks) before planning images — do not work from a summary or teaser snippet.

## STEP 2 — Break the chapter into scenes
- Identify sequential, distinct visual story beats covering the FULL chapter start to finish (dialogue moments, action beats, reactions, reveals, establishing shots) — roughly 6 scenes per image.
- No two panels in the whole chapter should depict the same pose/moment. Vary camera framing (close-up, wide, over-the-shoulder) like a real comic.
- Keep scenes in chronological story order across images (image 1 = opening scenes, last image = ending scenes).

## STEP 3 — Layout rules for every image
- **6 panels per image only** (arrange as a clean 3x2 or 2x3 grid with thin black borders) — not 16 anymore.
- Wide cinematic aspect ratio (16:9), landscape orientation.
- **Each panel must include a short text caption or dialogue snippet burned into the artwork** (xianxia-style caption box or speech bubble) summarizing/quoting that moment — keep captions short (under ~12 words), in the same language as the source chapter text (Hindi if chapter is in Hindi).
- xianxia  art style, cel-shaded, dramatic lighting, detailed backgrounds matching the chapter's setting.
- Scenes within one image must look different from each other — vary poses, camera angles, characters shown, no repeats.

## STEP 4 — Character consistency
Maintain the same character look across ALL chapters/images (update only when the story explicitly changes it):
- **Jiang Chen** — young male cultivator, sharp calm eyes, dark hair tied back, flowing dark blue-black robes, calm powerful aura.
- **Mu Gaoqi (Gaoki)** — younger male disciple, plain grey robes, earnest/anxious expressive face, weaker cultivation.
- Any other recurring named character introduced in a chapter (villains, sect elders, beasts, etc.) — invent a consistent visual description the first time they appear and reuse it in every subsequent panel/chapter they appear in.
- Note new important characters/creatures introduced in the current chapter at the top of the generation plan so their design stays consistent across that chapter's images.

## STEP 5 — Content safety (mandatory)
- Do NOT depict graphic gore, dismemberment, blood spray, explicit death, sexual content, or anything that violates image-generation content policy.
- Replace violent moments with stylized magical/energy-effect depictions (light flashes, sparks, glowing auras, magical binding, etc.) instead of literal injury.
- If a specific scene cannot be safely depicted even after softening, SKIP that scene entirely and move to the next one — do not force it. Mention skipped scenes briefly to the user at the end.
- If a generate_image call gets blocked by moderation, rewrite that single prompt with softer/less graphic wording and retry once before giving up on that panel.

## STEP 6 — How many images to generate
- Generate total of 10 images as the chapter needs to cover it fully at 9 scenes/image.
- Always issue all of that response's generate_image calls together (in parallel) rather than one at a time.

## STEP 7 — File naming & delivery
- Save files as `/home/user/chapter_images/chapter<N>_image<i>.png` (e.g. `chapter485_image1.png`).
- After generation, present all successfully generated images to the user (present_file), in order.
- Briefly summarize (1-2 lines) which part of the chapter each image covers, and note any skipped/softened scenes.
